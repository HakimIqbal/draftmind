# Landing Page Brief

Desc:
Spec for a marketing or product landing page: hero, value props, social proof, CTAs, and design direction. Hand this to an AI tool like v0, Cursor, or Lovable to generate a polished landing page.

Instructions: Write this as a creative brief that could be handed directly to a designer or AI page builder. Be specific about copy — write actual headlines, not placeholders. Value propositions should be benefit-focused, not feature-focused. Social proof should feel authentic. Design direction should reference specific visual styles, color palettes, or reference sites rather than vague adjectives like 'modern' or 'clean.' The page should have a clear conversion narrative from top to bottom.

Template                                          7 Sections 

## Page Goal & Audience
### Conversion Goal
The single primary action you want visitors to take: sign up, buy, book a demo, join waitlist, download, etc.

### Target Visitor
- **Traffic source**: Where they come from (paid ad, organic search, Product Hunt, referral, social)
- **Awareness level**: What they already know about you
- **Key question**: The #1 thing on their mind when they land
- **Primary objection**: What might stop them from converting

This profile shapes every section below — the page should feel like a direct answer to this visitor.

## Hero Section
The first thing visitors see — it must answer three questions instantly: What is this? Who is it for? Why should I care?

### Copy
- **Headline**: 8 words or fewer, benefit-driven (not clever — clear)
- **Subheadline**: 1-2 sentences expanding on the value proposition
- **CTA button**: Exact button text (e.g., 'Start free trial' not 'Submit')

### Visual
- **Hero image/visual**: Product screenshot, demo video, illustration, or animation
- **Layout**: Full-width, split (text left / visual right), centered, etc.
- **Above the fold**: Everything visible before scrolling

## Value Propositions
3-4 key benefits written from the user's perspective — what they GET, not what the product HAS.

### Format for Each
- **Headline**: 5-8 words, benefit-focused
- **Description**: 1-2 sentences explaining how the product delivers this benefit
- **Visual**: Icon suggestion, illustration, or screenshot

### Example
- **Ship 10x faster** — AI handles the boilerplate so you focus on what makes your product unique. No more copy-pasting patterns between projects.

Order from most compelling to least. Address the visitor's top concerns from the audience section above.

## Social Proof
What builds trust and credibility on this page. Be specific — vague praise doesn't convert.

### Options (pick 2-4)
- **Customer testimonials**: Real quotes with name, title, company, and photo
- **Logo bar**: Companies/brands that use the product
- **Usage stats**: '10,000+ teams,' '2M documents created,' etc.
- **Press mentions**: 'Featured in TechCrunch, Product Hunt #1 of the day'
- **Case study snippet**: 'Company X increased conversion by 40%'
- **Awards/badges**: G2, Product Hunt, industry awards

Order by persuasiveness. Lead with whatever is strongest.

## Feature Showcase
3-5 key features that demonstrate HOW the product delivers the value props above.

### Format for Each
- **Headline**: Benefit-oriented, not technical ('See changes in real time' not 'WebSocket integration')
- **Description**: 2-3 sentences on how it works and why it matters
- **Visual**: Screenshot, GIF, short video, or illustration
- **Layout**: Alternating left/right, tabbed showcase, or interactive demo

Order from most impressive to least. Each feature should build confidence that the product actually works.

## CTA & Conversion
### Primary CTA
- **Button copy**: Exact text (action-oriented: 'Start building free' not 'Get started')
- **Form fields**: Only what is absolutely required (email? name + email? just a button?)
- **Placement**: After hero, after features, sticky footer, etc.

### Secondary CTA
- **For hesitant visitors**: 'Watch demo,' 'See pricing,' 'Talk to sales'

### Trust Builders
- Risk reducers: 'No credit card required,' '14-day free trial,' 'Cancel anytime'
- Pricing preview: Show pricing or 'Free for up to X users'
- Urgency (if authentic): 'Early access pricing,' 'Limited beta spots'

## Design Direction
### Visual Style
- **Color palette**: Primary, secondary, accent (hex codes or descriptive: 'dark navy + electric blue accents')
- **Typography**: Sans-serif modern, serif editorial, monospace tech, etc.
- **Spacing**: Dense/information-rich vs. spacious/premium
- **Vibe**: Minimal, playful, corporate, developer-focused, luxury, etc.

### References
List 1-3 existing sites whose visual style to emulate and what specifically to borrow from each.

### Brand Assets
- Logo, brand fonts, brand colors that must be used
- Existing design system or component library

### Responsive
- Mobile-first or desktop-first
- Key mobile adaptations (collapsed nav, stacked layout, etc.)
- Animation and interaction preferences (subtle or expressive)
