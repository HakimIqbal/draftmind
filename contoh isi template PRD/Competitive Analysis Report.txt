# Competitive Analysis Report

Desc:
This template provides a comprehensive structure for analyzing and reporting on competitors, covering company background, product offerings, market positioning, and strategic insights.

Instructions: Go beyond surface-level feature comparison. Include specific pricing, positioning gaps, and strategic differences. Be honest about your own product's weaknesses relative to competitors. Customer sentiment should come from real reviews and data sources. Every insight should connect to an actionable product or strategy recommendation.

Template Sections                                                     5 sections

## Company Profiles
Background on each competitor being analyzed.

### Format for Each Competitor
**[Competitor Name]**
- **Overview**: What they do, company size, funding stage, headquarters
- **Founded**: Year and brief history
- **Revenue/Funding**: Publicly available financial signals
- **Key products**: Their main offerings and target market
- **Leadership**: Notable executives and their background
- **Recent moves**: Acquisitions, pivots, major launches in the last 12 months

### Your Company (for comparison)
Brief summary of your own positioning to anchor the analysis.
